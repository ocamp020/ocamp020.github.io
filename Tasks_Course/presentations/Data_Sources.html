Data and source acknowledgments

Occupational descriptions and exposure

This material uses information from the O*NET 27.2 Database by the U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration, developed by the National Center for O*NET Development, used under CC BY 4.0. O*NET is a trademark of the U.S. Department of Labor. See the database archive. Selected fields have been reorganized for the teaching interface.

Exposure labels and released occupation scores are from GPTs-are-GPTs. The repository license accompanies the saved example. The human beta aggregation was independently checked against all 923 released occupation scores.

The career exhibits combine archived O*NET knowledge and required-education ratings with the July 2024 CIP 2020 to O*NET-SOC 2019 education crosswalk. Seven program areas and 21 career examples are selected for this class. Program links describe preparation, not hiring demand or graduate placement. Eight new charts are calculations from public data; they do not update the paper’s original AI capability assumptions. Exhibit provenance and input hashes.

Adoption

Rates are from the RPS public occupation and task adoption indices, files generated October 19, 2026, pooling August and November 2025 and February and May 2026. The interface reorganizes unsuppressed published cells; it does not impute suppressed values. Rates are survey weighted and counts are unweighted. The task mapping uses O*NET 20.1; it has not been silently joined to the exposure paper's O*NET 27.2 data.

Nine career-focused charts use released cells or clearly identified published estimates. They cover 14 broad occupations and 15 work activities, plus employer encouragement, reported savings, industry use, and ChatGPT topic shares. N below 20 remains suppressed; cells with 20–49 observations are marked for caution. These are descriptive comparisons; no confidence intervals are inferred from N. Rapid Adoption uses 2024 survey waves, while the consumer ChatGPT study uses its separate 2024–25 platform sample. The source figure comparing implied and measured productivity is retained alongside the survey results. Exhibit provenance and input hashes.
